Elite Events in Bangkok — September 2026

From a five-week opera and dance marathon to a fight card at Muay Thai's spiritual home, Bangkok's September calendar moves faster than its skyline.

Published: September 1, 2026 Edition: September 2026 Verified Real Events Location: Bangkok, Thailand

Bangkok in September doesn't wait for high season to get serious. The city's culinary elite converge for the World Gourmet Festival's marquee dinners at the Anantara Siam, LNG executives from six continents fill BITEC for Gastech, and UOB LIVE sells out before the poster's even printed. Between the trade floors, the gem exchanges, and the temple air, this is a month that rewards knowing exactly where to be — and when.

Verified Major Events & Cultural Calendars

Here are the verified major public and private cultural, sporting, and social events taking place in Bangkok this September 2026:

📅 September 4, 2026 📍 Lumpinee Stadium

ONE Fight Night 47

ONE Championship returns to its spiritual home, Lumpinee Stadium, for a Prime Video primetime card blending Muay Thai, kickboxing and MMA. Watching ringside here means seeing the sport in the very stadium that helped define it.

📅 September 5 – October 17, 2026 📍 Thailand Cultural Centre

Bangkok's 28th International Festival of Dance & Music

Asia's largest performing-arts festival returns to the Thailand Cultural Centre, importing twelve full-scale opera, ballet and orchestral productions from nine countries — from Helikon Opera's La Traviata to the Bolshoi Theatre of Belarus's Romeo & Juliet. Six weeks of world-class programming in a city not typically associated with opera and classical dance.

📅 September 6, 2026 📍 UOB LIVE, EMSPHERE

Official HIGE DANdism Asia Tour 2026 in Bangkok

Japan's biggest pop-rock export plays its first-ever full concert in Thailand at UOB LIVE — already sold out well ahead of the date. A rare chance to catch a band that fills domes at home in an intimate 6,000-capacity room.

📅 September 8 – 12, 2026 📍 Queen Sirikit National Convention Center

74th Bangkok Gems & Jewelry Fair

Twice yearly, the Queen Sirikit National Convention Center becomes ground zero for the global gem trade, with 700 exhibitor booths of Thai-cut stones, fine jewelry and precious metals. It's a working reminder of why Bangkok remains one of the world's key jewelry manufacturing and trading hubs.

📅 September 10 – 13, 2026 📍 IMPACT Exhibition Center

Thailand Coffee Fest 2026

Southeast Asia's biggest coffee festival takes over IMPACT with 380-plus exhibitors, farm-to-cup workshops and a live barista championship arena. Four days that offer the clearest read on where Asian coffee culture is headed next.

📅 September 12, 2026 📍 The Fig Lobby Bangkok

Art Battle Bangkok

Painters go head-to-head on blank canvases in timed rounds at The Fig Lobby, with the crowd voting a live champion by night's end. Equal parts spectacle and genuine talent showcase — one of Bangkok's most theatrical evenings for contemporary art.

📅 September 14 – 17, 2026 📍 BITEC (Bangkok International Trade & Exhibition Centre)

Gastech 2026

The world's largest exhibition for natural gas, LNG and the energy transition lands at BITEC, drawing an expected 50,000 energy professionals and exhibitors from Chevron, ExxonMobil and Shell. A genuine bellwether for where global energy capital and policy are heading next.

📅 September 18, 2026 📍 Dusit Thani Bangkok

21st PropertyGuru Thailand Property Awards

Thailand's most prestigious real-estate industry gala convenes developers, architects and investors at the Dusit Thani for a night that decides who represents the country at December's Asia-wide final. The sharpest single read on where Thai luxury property is actually heading.

📅 September 22 – 24, 2026 📍 Centara Grand & Bangkok Convention Centre at CentralWorld

IT&CMA and CTW Asia-Pacific 2026

The region's premier MICE and corporate-travel gathering brings meeting planners, incentive-travel buyers and travel-management decision-makers to CentralWorld for three days of dealmaking. For anyone working in luxury events or business travel across Asia-Pacific, this is where the relationships get made.

📅 September 29 – October 3, 2026 📍 Anantara Siam Bangkok Hotel

World Gourmet Festival 2026

Bangkok's most storied culinary week returns to the Anantara Siam for its 26th edition, pairing more than two dozen Michelin-starred chefs — among them Julien Royer of Odette and Dave Pynt of Burnt Ends — with the city's top kitchens for one-night-only collaborations. Seats at the marquee dinners sell out fast; this is the closest Bangkok gets to a global culinary summit.
