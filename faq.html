Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about EliteLoop.

What is EliteLoop?

EliteLoop is a social discovery app for premium events, nearby plans, and badge-based access.

How does the badge system work?

There are 3 badge levels: Bronze, Silver, and Gold. Bronze is free. Silver and Gold unlock more visibility, access, and messaging power.

How is privacy ensured?

Your location is approximate, not exact. Personal information is protected with Apple or Google Sign-In and controlled profile sharing.

How can I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime from App Store or Play Store settings. Premium stays active until the end of the current billing period.

Which countries is it available in?

EliteLoop is designed for a global community with Turkish, English, Russian, Arabic, Chinese, and Japanese support.

Is EliteLoop available on Android?

Yes! EliteLoop is now live on both the App Store and Google Play.
