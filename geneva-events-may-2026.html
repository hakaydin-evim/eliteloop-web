The real networking happens during the private "Chatham House" breakout sessions in adjacent hotel suites.
  • AI in Watchmaking Roundtable · May 18: A strictly invitation-only gathering of industry titans from the Richemont and LVMH groups, discussing the intersection of heritage craftsmanship and predictive AI. The social spillover at Quai des Bergues following the event is the month's highest-signal node for luxury tech.
