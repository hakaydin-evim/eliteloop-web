FAQ

Frequently asked questions

EliteLoop is a premium social discovery app for exclusive events, nearby plans, and badge-gated connections. It helps selective individuals network and make real-life plans with high intent and zero noise.
We use a 3-badge level system (Bronze, Silver, and Gold) to verify status and gate visibility. Bronze is free. Silver and Gold unlock features like direct messaging, seeing nearby user names, photos, and Gold-only AI event recommendations.
Your exact location is never shared. The app uses fuzzy location radars (approximate radius) on the map. You have full control over who sees your profile and your badge tier controls all matching requests.
You can cancel your subscription at any time directly through your App Store or Google Play account settings. If cancelled, your access continues until the end of your billing cycle.
EliteLoop is designed for a global, selective community and is available worldwide. The app supports English, Turkish, Russian, Arabic, Chinese, and Japanese.
Yes! EliteLoop is fully live and available for both iOS (App Store) and Android (Google Play Store) devices.
