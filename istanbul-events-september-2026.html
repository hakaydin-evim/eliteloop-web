Elite Events in Istanbul — September 2026

Contemporary Istanbul 2026, the Bosphorus Cup Regatta, and the Akbank Jazz Festival.

Published: September 1, 2026 Edition: September 2026 Verified Real Events Location: Istanbul, Turkey

September 2026 brings a cultural crescendo to Istanbul. Whether you are navigating contemporary art fairs, exclusive regattas on the Bosphorus, or the city's celebrated dining and cocktail festivals, knowing where the city's key gathering points are is essential.

Verified Major Events & Cultural Calendars

Here are the verified major public and private cultural, sporting, and social events taking place in Istanbul this September 2026:

📅 September 3 – 5, 2026 📍 Istanbul Haliç Congress Center

Istanbul Global E-Export Summit (IGEXX)

Backed by the Ministry of Trade and the Exporters' Assembly, IGEXX pulls cross-border e-commerce operators, logistics and payments players, and international investors into three days of B2B matchmaking at Haliç Congress Center — real deal flow, not just panels.

📅 September 5 – 6, 2026 📍 Beykoz Kundura

Taste and the City

Beykoz Kundura's industrial-chic halls host chefs, producers, and live music treating food as a shared, talked-through experience rather than a transaction — a more considered register than the city's usual food-festival format.

📅 September 9, 2026 📍 Ora Arena

Ebi Live in Istanbul

One of Persian pop's most enduring voices takes over Ora Arena for a rare live date — a genuine draw for Istanbul's sizable Iranian community and anyone after a proper arena show built on decades of hits rather than a single new record.

📅 September 9 – 10, 2026 📍 Istanbul Expo Center

GITEX AI Türkiye

The GITEX brand's first Turkish edition lands at Istanbul Expo Center with 300-plus enterprise and startup exhibitors, government policymakers, and investors in the room — a fast read on how aggressively Türkiye is courting the global AI industry.

📅 September 10 – 13, 2026 📍 Tepe Nautilus

Istanbul Coffee Festival 2026

The city's biggest specialty-coffee gathering brings world-class roasters, barista championships and tasting workshops to the Asian side for four days. A magnet for Istanbul's design-and-food crowd and an easy, stylish daytime meetup.

📅 September 16 – 19, 2026 📍 Kalamis Marina

Bosphorus Cup 2026 Regatta

Istanbul's landmark 25th-anniversary regatta, the only race in the world for which the Bosphorus strait is closed to commercial shipping so the fleet can sail it end to end. Yacht-club energy from Kalamis to Buyukada, with prime waterfront viewing along the strait.

📅 September 19 – 20, 2026 📍 LifePark

Istanbul Cocktail Festival

The city's best bars and bartenders take their programs outdoors for two open-air days of cocktails, live sets, and workshops — as close as Istanbul gets to a proper showcase of its fast-improving drinks culture.

📅 September 23 – 27, 2026 📍 Tersane Istanbul

Contemporary Istanbul 2026

The city's flagship contemporary art fair returns to the restored Ottoman shipyards of Tersane, drawing 70+ galleries from over 20 countries plus international collectors and curators. The definitive week for Istanbul's art-world set.

📅 September 23 – 26, 2026 📍 Istanbul Expo Center

MÜSİAD EXPO 2026

Held every two years, this is Türkiye's broadest cross-sector trade fair — 350-plus exhibitors and an expected 50,000 visitors under one roof at Istanbul Expo Center, offering one of the clearest single snapshots of where Turkish business is headed.

📅 September 25, 2026 📍 Ataköy Marina Açık Hava

Black Coffee at Ataköy Marina

The Grammy-winning Afro-house heavyweight headlines an open-air night on the Marmara waterfront — the kind of single-name booking that turns Ataköy Marina into the city's most-talked-about dance floor for one night.

📅 September 26 – 27, 2026 📍 Zorlu PSM

36th Akbank Jazz Festival — Opening Weekend

One of Europe's longest-running jazz festivals opens with British keyboardist Joe Armon-Jones on September 26 and drummer Momoko Gill on September 27, launching 30+ concerts across Zorlu PSM, Babylon, Salon IKSV and historic venues through October 11.

📅 September 26, 2026 📍 KüçükÇiftlik Park

Boris Brejcha: Reflections Tour

Istanbul's high-tech-minimal faithful get the full production this time — a three-hour set plus the elaborate visual show built for the Reflections tour — at what's become one of the city's most reliably sold-out electronic nights.

📅 September 26 – 27, 2026 📍 LifePark

Istanbul Oktoberfest

LifePark's forested grounds host the city's biggest autumn beer-and-brass blowout — long tables, steins, and two days of live sets from Turkish rock acts including Gripin and Son Feci Bisiklet — loud, unpretentious, and unmistakably Istanbul's take on the tradition.

📅 September 27 – 28, 2026 📍 Harbiye Oditoryumu

Sami Yusuf: Roots — Live in Istanbul

Sami Yusuf handpicked Harbiye Oditoryumu himself for its acoustics, staging a newly conceived program — new arrangements, immersive visuals — across two nights that lean toward reflection and craft over spectacle.
