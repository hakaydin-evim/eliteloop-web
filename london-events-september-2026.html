Elite Events in London — September 2026

London Fashion Week SS27, Frieze Sculpture in Regent's Park, and the Royal Opera's season-opening Don Giovanni.

Published: September 1, 2026 Edition: September 2026 Verified Real Events Location: London, UK

September 2026 signals the start of a new season across London. Whether you are navigating fashion week previews, landmark gallery and opera openings, or championship-level sport at The O2, knowing where the city's key gathering points are is essential.

Verified Major Events & Cultural Calendars

Here are the verified major public and private cultural, sporting, and social events taking place in London this September 2026:

📅 September 3 – 6, 2026 📍 Tobacco Dock

Meatopia On-Fire

The UK's original live-fire food festival brings up to 75 chefs to Tobacco Dock to cook exclusive dishes over wood and charcoal, alongside live music and premium bars. The benchmark carnivore event of London's food calendar, four days running.

📅 September 5 – October 24, 2026 📍 Royal Court Theatre

Man to Man, starring Tilda Swinton

Tilda Swinton returns to the solo role that launched her career at the Royal Court nearly 40 years ago, in a landmark revival of Manfred Karge's one-woman show before it transfers to Berlin. A rare chance to see a genuine screen icon on a London stage in an intimate 90-seat house.

📅 September 10 – October 15, 2026 📍 Royal Opera House

Don Giovanni opens the Royal Opera 2026/27 season

Mozart's darkest opera opens the Royal Ballet and Opera's new season on the Covent Garden main stage in Kasper Holten's acclaimed staging. Opening-week performances at the Royal Opera House are among London's most coveted cultural invitations.

📅 September 10, 2026 – July 11, 2027 📍 British Museum

The Bayeux Tapestry

The 70-metre, 900-year-old embroidery leaves France for the first time in nearly a millennium, on loan from Bayeux to the British Museum in a historic Anglo-French exchange. A once-in-a-generation blockbuster — expect the year's hardest-to-get museum ticket in London.

📅 September 12 – 20, 2026 📍 Design districts across London

London Design Festival 2026

The 24th edition of London's citywide design festival fills nine days with installations, showroom openings, talks and launch parties across the capital's design districts. A magnet for architects, collectors and the creative industries.

📅 September 12, 2026 📍 Ministry of Sound

Bonkers: 30 Years

The hardcore-and-hardstyle institution takes over Ministry of Sound's every room to mark three decades, after five straight sold-out anniversary parties. A rite of passage for London's rave scene, in the club that built it.

📅 September 12 – 13, 2026 📍 The Cause

Waterworks Extended

Forced out of Gunnersbury Park by a planning-permission delay, Waterworks regroups as a defiant 36-hour continuous takeover of The Cause — seven spaces, outdoor by day and indoor by night, with sets from Erol Alkan, Modeselektor, Midland and Eris Drew. The scrappy relocation only sharpens the draw for London's most respected underground electronic weekend.

📅 September 16 – November 1, 2026 📍 English Gardens, The Regent's Park

Frieze Sculpture 2026

Frieze transforms the English Gardens of The Regent's Park into an open-air gallery with major works by eleven international artists, curated by Fatos Ustek. The opening in mid-September marks the start of London's autumn art-world season ahead of Frieze London.

📅 September 17 – 21, 2026 📍 Various venues, central London

London Fashion Week SS27

The British Fashion Council's flagship week brings the global fashion industry to London for the Spring/Summer 2027 collections. Five days of runway shows, presentations and industry parties make it the city's densest networking moment of the season.

📅 September 19 – 20, 2026 📍 Copper Box Arena

Davis Cup: Great Britain vs Ecuador

Cam Norrie and Wimbledon doubles champion Henry Patten lead the Lexus GB team in a Davis Cup qualifier that decides a place in November's Final 8 — the squad's first-ever tie at Copper Box Arena. Genuine national-team stakes, in an arena built for atmosphere.

📅 September 23, 2026 – January 31, 2027 📍 Serpentine North Gallery

Amar Kanwar at Serpentine

The acclaimed Indian filmmaker transforms Serpentine North into an immersive, meditative environment of new and existing film works exploring displacement, memory and nationalism. Free entry to one of London's most quietly powerful galleries, on the edge of Kensington Gardens.

📅 September 23 – 24, 2026 📍 Olympia London

Big Data LDN 2026

The UK's leading data, analytics and AI conference draws 300-plus speakers and 200-plus exhibitors to Olympia for two days of real-world case studies on building AI-driven enterprises. The essential annual gathering for London's data and AI leadership.

📅 September 23 – 24, 2026 📍 ExCeL London

eCommerce Expo 2026

Over 10,000 B2B and B2C ecommerce professionals converge on ExCeL for two days of AI-driven retail strategy, sustainable fulfilment and growth tactics from 200-plus solution providers. A high-density trading floor for anyone building or scaling a retail brand.

📅 September 25 – 27, 2026 📍 The O2

Laver Cup 2026

Team Europe, captained by Yannick Noah, faces Team World under Andre Agassi in tennis's marquee team event, returning to The O2 for its ninth edition. Three nights of the sport's biggest names in a format built for spectacle over ranking points.

📅 September 26, 2026 📍 KOKO

Sigma at KOKO

The drum and bass duo behind 'Nobody to Love' take over KOKO's Camden landmark for a full-throttle daytime headline show, following back-to-back London sellouts. A festival-grade set inside one of the capital's most storied rooms.
