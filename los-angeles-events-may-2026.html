Elite Events in Los Angeles May 2026

A field guide to LA's May social landscape: finance summits, private member exclusives, and the entertainment social circuit.

May 9, 2026 · 3 verified event signals · EliteLoop Editorial

Los Angeles in May is defined by the convergence of the entertainment industry's social season and the spring finance summits. As the city warms, the social layer moves from the private studios of Burbank to the rooftops of West Hollywood and the expansive estates of the Bird Streets. This is a city built on the illusion of access, but the actual power structures remain deeply private.

While the sunset strip remains loud and public, the real decisions are being made in the quiet corners of San Vicente Bungalows, at the invitation-only dinners surrounding the Milken Conference, and the closed-door Silicon Beach tech mixers. This month, access to the right room means separating yourself from the noise of the public circuit.

Finance & The Milken Circuit

May kicks off with the Milken Institute Global Conference, transforming Beverly Hills into a hub of global capital. The real networking, however, happens off-site.

  • Milken Institute Private Dinners (May 4-7, Beverly Hills): The main conference is a public anchor, but the real access points are the private dinner series hosted by LPs and GPs across Beverly Hills. These are the rooms where global capital actually aligns.
  • Family Office & Wealth Mixers (May 6, Century City): Ultra-exclusive afternoon sessions dedicated to wealth preservation and off-market real estate discussions, often held in private boardrooms overlooking the city.
  • Post-Conference Rooftop Socials (May 7, West Hollywood): Evening wind-down events where finance meets entertainment. These mixers are heavily gated, requiring either a direct invite or a verified EliteLoop connection.

Tech & Silicon Beach

The migration of tech talent to Los Angeles has turned Santa Monica and Venice into powerhouses of private capital. Here are the rooms you need to be in.

  • Upfront Summit Social Circuit (May 12-14, Santa Monica): As venture capital descends on Santa Monica, the beachfront social gatherings serve as the primary discovery layer for the Silicon Beach elite. Expect founders, VCs, and tech-focused celebrities.
  • AI & Entertainment Mixer (May 15, Culver City): A late-evening mixer connecting artificial intelligence innovators with traditional studio executives. The crossover between tech and storytelling happens here.
  • Venice Beach Founder's Dinner (May 20, Venice): An intimate, high-stakes gathering for tech founders looking for unlisted opportunities and quiet funding rounds.
"In Los Angeles, access is not about being on the list; it is about being in the conversation that created the list."

The Private Member Socials

The true pulse of LA's elite lifestyle is dictated by its private members' clubs, which guard their exclusivity with extreme prejudice.

  • San Vicente Bungalows - Spring Socials (May Seasonal, West Hollywood): LA's most selective private member club hosts its May social series. Access is strictly through existing circles, making it the city's highest-filtered room of the month.
  • Soho House Terrace Series (May Weekends, West Hollywood & Malibu): The classic LA networking scene, shifting between the hills of West Hollywood and the beachfront at Little Beach House Malibu.
  • Bird Streets Private Gatherings (Late May, Hollywood Hills): Invitation-only estate parties where the city's highest-net-worth individuals gather away from the public eye.

Los Angeles's elite calendar for May 2026 is dense, but the doors are highly selective. Public RSVPs will only get you so far. Securing your place in these rooms requires the right badge, the right introduction, and the right network. It's time to move beyond the velvet rope and into the circle that matters.
