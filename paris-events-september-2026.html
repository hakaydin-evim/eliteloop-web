Elite Events in Paris — September 2026

Paris Fashion Week Womenswear SS27, FAB Paris at the Grand Palais, and Cézanne et nous.

Published: September 1, 2026 Edition: September 2026 Verified Real Events Location: Paris, France

September 2026 brings a cultured intensity to Paris, as fashion, design, and fine art claim the city's grandest stages. Whether you are navigating fashion week showrooms, gallery openings at the Grand Palais, orchestral season premieres, or the city's finest gastronomic gatherings, knowing where the city's key gathering points are is essential.

Verified Major Events & Cultural Calendars

Here are the verified major public and private cultural, sporting, and social events taking place in Paris this September 2026:

📅 September 3, 2026 📍 Philharmonie de Paris, Grande salle Pierre Boulez

Royal Concertgebouw Orchestra opens the Philharmonie season

Amsterdam's Royal Concertgebouw Orchestra launches the Philharmonie's Prem's festival with Santtu-Matias Rouvali conducting and Vikingur Olafsson playing Beethoven's Emperor Concerto. One of the world's top orchestras on the season's very first weekend makes this the classical date to claim early.

📅 September 5 – 7, 2026 📍 Paris Expo Porte de Versailles

Bijorhca

France's dedicated trade show for fine jewellery, watchmaking, and precious accessories brings roughly 500 exhibitors and tens of thousands of international buyers to Porte de Versailles. It's where next year's jewellery cases actually get decided, not just displayed.

📅 September 9 – 19, 2026 📍 Palais Garnier

Black Pearl: Meditations for Josephine at Palais Garnier

Peter Sellars stages a new tribute to Josephine Baker with soprano Julia Bullock, opening the Paris Opera's 2026/27 season under Garnier's gilded ceilings. A short 10-day run of a world-class premiere - exactly the kind of night that sells out to the city's cultural insiders.

📅 September 10 – 19, 2026 📍 Various venues across Paris design districts

Paris Design Week

A citywide design festival with 500 participants across 320 showrooms, galleries and studios in Paris's four design districts, running alongside the Maison&Objet trade fair. Evening openings and the Factory showcase of 150 emerging designers in the Marais make it a natural circuit for design-minded members.

📅 September 10 – 13, 2026 📍 Place de la Concorde

Village International de la Gastronomie

Place de la Concorde becomes a four-day culinary atlas, with kitchens from more than 80 countries and Italy as this year's guest of honor, presented under Alain Ducasse's sponsorship. It's a food event built for wandering and grazing rather than a single tasting menu.

📅 September 15 – 17, 2026 📍 Paris Expo Porte de Versailles

NRF Retail's Big Show Europe

Formerly Paris Retail Week, this is the European counterpart to New York's flagship NRF show — three days, 15,000 attendees and roughly 480 exhibitors at Porte de Versailles, reading where stores and commerce are actually headed next.

📅 September 15 – 16, 2026 📍 Paris Expo Porte de Versailles

Big Data & AI Paris

France's reference gathering for data and AI decision-makers moves earlier into mid-September for its 15th edition, drawing thousands of practitioners to Porte de Versailles for two days on deploying AI at enterprise scale. High-signal over hyped — this is where the deployments get discussed, not just the demos.

📅 September 17, 2026 📍 Accor Arena

Evanescence at Accor Arena

Evanescence bring their 2026 world tour to Bercy for a single Paris date, joined by Poppy and Nova Twins — a rare arena-scale rock night on a stage usually reserved for pop and hip-hop. Seventeen thousand seats, one set built around two decades of gothic-rock anthems.

📅 September 19 – 23, 2026 📍 Grand Palais

FAB Paris at the Grand Palais

The fusion of Fine Arts Paris and the Biennale des Antiquaires brings around a hundred galleries spanning antiquity to contemporary art, jewelry and design under the Grand Palais nave. It is the collector-grade art event of the Paris rentree, open to the public as well as the trade.

📅 September 19, 2026 📍 Place de la Bastille

Techno Parade

For its 26th edition, around 200 DJs and a dozen sound-system floats roll from Place de la Bastille to Nation, with Detroit — techno's birthplace — as this year's guest of honor. Free and unticketed, it's the day Paris's electronic scene claims the street before scattering into club afterparties citywide.

📅 September 23, 2026 – January 17, 2027 📍 Grand Palais

Cézanne et nous

Nearly 180 works trace how Cézanne rewired modern art through the artists who claimed him — Matisse, Picasso, Mondrian, Bridget Riley, Peter Doig — in a co-production spanning the Musée d'Orsay, Centre Pompidou, and Orangerie collections. It's the season's defining museum opening, staged under the Grand Palais's restored glass nave.

📅 September 24 – 27, 2026 📍 Grande Halle de la Villette

Nightclubbing Festival

DJ Jen Cardini debuts Nightclubbing, a four-day festival tracing electronic music's political lineage — Chicago house, Detroit techno, Europe's free-party scene — through club nights, talks, and installations anchored at La Villette. First editions rarely stay this scaled-down; this is the one to catch early.

📅 September 26 – 28, 2026 📍 Grande Halle de la Villette

Cocktail Street

Cocktail Street turns the Grande Halle de la Villette into a 4,500-square-metre bar crawl for its tenth-anniversary edition, with nearly 50 bars poured by houses like Hennessy, Fever-Tree, and Dolin, Radio Nova on the decks, and a street-food market alongside. It's the single biggest mixology gathering on the Paris calendar.

📅 September 27, 2026 📍 Eiffel Tower (Quai Jacques Chirac)

La Grande Classique (Paris-Versailles)

The 47th La Grande Classique sends 25,000 runners 16.2 kilometres from the foot of the Eiffel Tower through the Meudon forest to the gates of the Château de Versailles — France's classic point-to-point road race. This year's bibs sold out in under four days.

📅 September 28 – 30, 2026 📍 Various venues across Paris

Paris Fashion Week - Womenswear Spring/Summer 2027

The FHCM's official womenswear week opens September 28 with 101 houses on the calendar, from Julie Kegels's opener to Louis Vuitton's closing show. Shows are invitation-only, but the week floods the city's hotels, restaurants and galleries with the global fashion set - prime terrain for high-signal networking.
