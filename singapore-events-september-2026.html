Elite Events in Singapore — September 2026

Post Malone's Stadium World Tour, the Singapore Tennis Open, and SuperReturn Asia 2026.

Published: September 1, 2026 Edition: September 2026 Verified Real Events Location: Singapore, Singapore

September 2026 brings an electric energy to Singapore. Whether you are navigating stadium-scale concerts, top-tier WTA tennis, global finance summits, or landmark design showcases, knowing where the city's key gathering points are is essential.

Verified Major Events & Cultural Calendars

Here are the verified major public and private cultural, sporting, and social events taking place in Singapore this September 2026:

📅 September 1 – 6, 2026 📍 Marina Bay Sands

Beyond the Cellar — Marina Bay Sands Wine Festival Finale

Marina Bay Sands' inaugural wine festival turns the resort into a living cellar across 20 venues, with curated pours, masterclasses and wine dinners at Michelin-starred tables like CUT, Wakuda and Maison Boulud. The final week (through 6 September) is the moment to be seen swirling a glass 57 storeys above the bay.

📅 September 3 – 6, 2026 📍 Esplanade – Theatres on the Bay

Baybeats 2026

Singapore's longest-running independent music festival hits its 25th edition with 47 bands across five stages over four free days on the bay — the clearest read on where the region's alternative and indie scenes stand right now, from homegrown acts to touring names like Boris and Bilderbuch.

📅 September 12, 2026 📍 Zouk Singapore

Elderbrook Live at Zouk

British electronic artist Elderbrook — the voice and production behind global dance-floor staples — headlines Zouk's main room for one night, turning the club's flagship space into the city's most in-demand floor for that Saturday.

📅 September 18 – 20, 2026 📍 Suntec Singapore Convention & Exhibition Centre

HomeFest Interior Design Festival 2026

Singapore's largest home festival gathers over 300 accredited interior designers and 50-plus home and lifestyle brands under one roof, with live design consultations and daily masterclasses — the definitive stop for anyone furnishing or renovating this year.

📅 September 20, 2026 📍 Esplanade Concert Hall

Belle and Sebastian: If You're Feeling Sinister — 30th Anniversary

The Scottish indie-pop institution performs their landmark album in full for its 30th anniversary, alongside a set of fan favourites — a rare complete-album show from a band whose influence on a generation of guitar pop is hard to overstate.

📅 September 21 – 27, 2026 📍 OCBC Arena

Singapore Tennis Open 2026 (WTA 500)

Newly upgraded to WTA 500 status — one of only 18 such events worldwide and Southeast Asia's sole tournament at this tier — the Singapore Tennis Open brings a US$1.2M field of world-class players to indoor hard courts at the OCBC Arena. Finals weekend is the city's premier courtside social fixture this month.

📅 September 21, 2026 📍 Foo Chow Hall

Ash: 1977 — 30th Anniversary Tour

Northern Irish rock trio Ash tear through 1977 — the album behind Girl from Mars — to mark its 30th anniversary, trading festival stages for an intimate hall setting that makes this a far more personal booking than their usual arena slots.

📅 September 23 – 24, 2026 📍 Sands Expo & Convention Centre

Passenger Terminal Expo Asia 2026

The Passenger Terminal Expo makes its Asia debut in Singapore, bringing airport operators, airlines and technology providers together for the region's first dedicated look at the future of the airport passenger experience — 125-plus exhibitors and 3,500-plus industry professionals in one hall.

📅 September 25, 2026 📍 National Stadium

Post Malone: The BIG ASS Stadium World Tour

Post Malone brings his stadium tour to Singapore for a single night at the National Stadium — one of the year's biggest ticketed music events in the city, already moving fast despite a from-$138 entry point.

📅 September 26 – 30, 2026 📍 National Design Centre

Singapore Design Week 2026

The final edition of Singapore Design Week before it relaunches as the Singapore Design Biennale in 2027, running 26 September to 4 October with the National Design Centre as its hub and showcases across Bras Basah.Bugis, Marina, Orchard and Singapore Science Park. Expect exhibition openings, designer talks and a marketplace drawing the region's creative set.

📅 September 28 – 30, 2026 📍 Marina Bay Sands Convention Centre

SuperReturn Asia 2026

Asia's premier private capital summit convenes 2,500+ senior decision-makers — 1,200+ LPs and 1,000+ GPs from 50+ countries — at Marina Bay Sands Convention Centre from 28 September to 1 October. The highest-density networking week of the season for private equity, venture and private credit circles in Singapore.

📅 September 29 – 30, 2026 📍 Sands Expo & Convention Centre

Tech Week Singapore 2026

More than 28,000 attendees and 550-plus speakers converge for Southeast Asia's largest enterprise-technology gathering, pulling cloud architects, cybersecurity leads and infrastructure decision-makers from across Asia-Pacific into two days of dealmaking and product reveals.
