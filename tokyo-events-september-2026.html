Elite Events in Tokyo — September 2026

Rakuten Fashion Week Tokyo, the Aki Basho Sumo Tournament, and Ultra Japan 2026.

Published: September 1, 2026 Edition: September 2026 Verified Real Events Location: Tokyo, Japan

September 2026 brings a refined intensity to Tokyo. Whether you are navigating front-row fashion weeks, centuries-old sumo tournaments, landmark orchestral evenings, or electronic nights beneath Odaiba's skyline, knowing where the city's key gathering points are is essential.

Verified Major Events & Cultural Calendars

Here are the verified major public and private cultural, sporting, and social events taking place in Tokyo this September 2026:

📅 September 1 – 5, 2026 📍 Shibuya Hikarie

Rakuten Fashion Week TOKYO 2027 S/S

Japan Fashion Week's flagship runway season takes over Shibuya Hikarie, drawing designers, buyers and press for a week of shows and industry parties. The prime front-row-and-afterparty moment on Tokyo's early-autumn calendar.

📅 September 2 – 4, 2026 📍 Tokyo Big Sight

Tokyo International Gift Show Autumn 2026

Now in its 102nd edition, Japan's largest trade show for design, lifestyle and gift-sector buyers fills every hall of Big Sight with next season's product lines before they reach retail. The definitive read on where Japanese consumer design is heading, and a serious sourcing floor for anyone in the trade.

📅 September 3 – 6, 2026 📍 Hibiya Park (Nirenoki Plaza)

Ya-Taiwan Festival 2026

Hibiya Park's first Taiwanese night-market takeover brings beef noodle soup, jipai fried chicken and roast duck to central Tokyo, capped nightly by a Shifen-style sky-lantern ritual. A rare, precisely dated chance to eat your way through Taipei's street-food canon without leaving the city.

📅 September 7 – 9, 2026 📍 Bar Trench

Negroni Week Tokyo

The global cocktail-world fundraiser lands in Tokyo with a Japanese twist, pairing barley shochu into the classic recipe as bars citywide pour their own takes for a good cause. It opens with a three-night pop-up at Ebisu's Bar Trench, showcasing renowned bartenders from across Japan before the citywide week begins.

📅 September 9 – 30, 2026 📍 The National Art Center, Tokyo

Masterpieces of the Renaissance from the Musée du Louvre

The National Art Center opens a blockbuster loan show of 50-plus Renaissance works from the Louvre, headlined by Leonardo da Vinci's La Belle Ferronnière on its first-ever visit to Japan. Opening weeks are the moment to see it before the crowds peak.

📅 September 9, 2026 📍 Spotify O-East

Adrian Sherwood Presents Dub Sessions 2026

On-U Sound founder Adrian Sherwood brings his long-running Dub Sessions series back to Shibuya for its 21st Tokyo outing, facing off against Nightmares on Wax to mark the twentieth anniversary of their landmark remix project. A serious night for serious sound-system heads, staged in one of the city's best-tuned rooms.

📅 September 13 – 27, 2026 📍 Ryogoku Kokugikan

September Grand Sumo Tournament (Aki Basho)

Fifteen days of top-division sumo at the Ryogoku Kokugikan, Tokyo's most atmospheric spectator ritual — box seats near the dohyo sell out within hours of release. The final weekend, when the championship is decided, is the one to be seen at.

📅 September 16 – October 25, 2026 📍 Yurakucho Museum

MAPPA EXPO: 15th Anniversary Exhibition

The studio behind Jujutsu Kaisen, Chainsaw Man and Attack on Titan's finale marks fifteen years with an immersive retrospective spanning 23 titles — life-size Titan installations, an illuminated Chainsaw Man set piece, and original production art. The first exhibition of this scale dedicated to the studio, and essential viewing for anyone tracking where anime culture meets high-production spectacle.

📅 September 17 – 21, 2026 📍 Makuhari Messe

Tokyo Game Show 2026

The gaming industry's most influential trade show expands to an unprecedented five-day run this year, with Japan's biggest publishers unveiling next-generation titles across every hall of Makuhari Messe. It's the single most important week on the calendar for anyone tracking where interactive entertainment is headed.

📅 September 19 – 20, 2026 📍 Ultra Park Odaiba

Ultra Japan 2026

Asia's flagship instalment of the global Ultra festival franchise takes over Odaiba's waterfront for two nights of world-class electronic acts and a production scale nothing else in the city attempts. It's Tokyo's biggest outdoor dance-music date of the year, and the one international crowds fly in specifically for.

📅 September 19, 2026 📍 Shinjuku Loft

Loft 50th Anniversary: The Fiendish Return of Balzac

Tokyo's most storied live house marks fifty years with the return of horror-punk veterans Balzac for a milestone one-night set, coinciding with the band's own thirtieth anniversary at the venue. A rare convergence of underground music history and a genuine Shinjuku institution.

📅 September 28 – 29, 2026 📍 Suntory Hall

London Symphony Orchestra at Suntory Hall — Pappano conducts Mahler 2

Sir Antonio Pappano leads the LSO in the world premiere of Dai Fujikura's Amber Alchemy followed by Mahler's Resurrection Symphony, with a second night of Bruckner 9 and violinist HIMARI. The classical highlight of Tokyo's September, with S-seats at ¥39,000.
